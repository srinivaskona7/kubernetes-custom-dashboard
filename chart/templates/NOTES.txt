=== Narrative TLS ===

Certificates are auto-provisioned by the Narrative CSI driver. The pod mounts
them at /tls. No NarrativeIssuer CRD is shipped in this chart — apply
scripts/narrative-issuer.yaml manually with admin kubectl context once per
namespace before the first deploy. The cert is the service-account pod
identity: aprn:apple:aws:us-west-2:<account>:eks-service-account:<cluster>/{{ .Release.Namespace }}/{{ include "btp-kyma-fleet-dashboard.serviceAccountName" . }}
Grant that APRN read access on the Whisper secret.
